# whack-a-mole

A game of whack-a-mole that lasts 30 seconds. The goal is to whack as many moles as possible:

- 3 points for catching a mole in less than 0.5 seconds
- 2 points for catching a mole in less than 1 second
- 1 point for catching a mole in less than 1.5 seconds
- or -1 point for missing a mole
